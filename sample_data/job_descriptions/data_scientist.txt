Data Scientist
Location: Gurugram | Experience: 2-4 years

Our retail analytics team builds demand-forecasting and customer-insight models for 1,200 stores. We are hiring a Data Scientist to build, evaluate and deploy machine learning models, and increasingly to apply LLMs to customer feedback.

Responsibilities
- Explore large datasets and turn business questions into analyses
- Build, validate and deploy machine learning models
- Design experiments and measure impact with sound statistics
- Communicate findings to business stakeholders with clear visualisations

Required skills
- 2+ years of hands-on experience in data science or machine learning
- Python with pandas, NumPy and scikit-learn
- Strong SQL
- Solid statistics: hypothesis testing, regression, probability
- Machine learning: model selection, feature engineering, evaluation

Preferred
- Deep learning with TensorFlow or PyTorch
- NLP and LLMs (Hugging Face, prompt engineering, RAG)
- Experience deploying models with Docker or on AWS
- Tableau or Power BI dashboards
- Master's degree in Statistics, Computer Science or a related field
